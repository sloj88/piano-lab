# Chapter 01 Reinforcement Specification — Meet the Piano

Status: v0.1 implementation-ready content proposal
Scope: Unnumbered supports for Sessions 001–024

## Purpose

Reinforcement is part of the 24-session journey, not a remedial side path and not extra numbered curriculum. Each card below gives a learner one short repeat, one simpler prerequisite, an observable readiness behavior, and a later retrieval point. The cards are deliberately small enough to use inside a core session, after choosing **Practicing** or **Stuck**, or during a voluntary revisit.

Reinforcement never changes the learner’s current sequential position unless the learner explicitly chooses to remain at a session. It never removes a completion record. Understanding, playing ability, readiness, completion history, and current position remain separate data.

## State behavior

### Ready

Show the observable readiness behavior in plain language, then offer **Continue**, **Keep Playing**, and **Repeat once**. Ready is self-reported; the application does not score the physical instrument.

### Practicing

Offer the skill’s short repeat card immediately. After the repeat, ask only the relevant understanding and playing reflection again. Keep the current session available; let the learner continue by choice.

### Stuck

Offer the simpler prerequisite first, with a named earlier session link. Explain what has been simplified—fewer notes, one hand, no notation, or slower coordination. Do not prescribe repeated full runs. If discomfort is the reason, instruct the learner to stop the physical task and seek qualified guidance rather than working through pain.

### Revisit

Opening an earlier session or reinforcement card must preserve:

- the furthest current sequential position;
- every prior session completion;
- separate understanding and playing reflections;
- the most recent Ready/Practicing/Stuck choice per session; and
- optional reinforcement history, if stored.

Completing a reinforcement card may be recorded as a helpful event, but it must not become a gate, score, streak, or additional session number.

## Reinforcement cards

### RF-KG-1 — Keyboard groups and C landmarks

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 001; retrieve 002, 008, 011, 023, 024.
- **Short repeat:** Find three groups of two black keys. For each, point to the white key immediately left and say “C.” Choose the C nearest the instrument’s middle and play it once.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Cover most of the keyboard so only one two-black-key group and nearby white keys remain. Touch the group, slide to its left edge, and point to C without playing.
- **Readiness indicator:** Finds C beside two different two-black-key groups without counting white keys from an end; identifies C4 with the size-dependent center explanation available.
- **Later review:** Treble C4 in 008, lower C3 in 011, then no-hint retrieval in 023–024.

### RF-RH-1 — Right-hand five-note position and control

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 001; practice 004; apply 009, 010, 020–024.
- **Short repeat:** Place RH 1–5 on C4–G4, play C4–D4–E4–F4–G4 and back once per steady beat, then release the hand completely.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Rest RH 1 on C4 and 2 on D4. Alternate C4/D4 for four slow beats without moving the wrist sideways.
- **Readiness indicator:** Plays K001 with the intended fingers, no extra turn-around pause, and no pain or persistent visible tension.
- **Later review:** Treble cards in 009, P01 in 010, M001 in 020, and milestones 023–024.

### RF-EAR-1 — Same and different pitch

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 002; return 007, 012, 020, 023–024.
- **Short repeat:** Hear one E001 pair, replay once, choose Same/Different, reveal the notes, and play both versions on the physical keys.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Compare two single-note pairs: C4/C4 and C4/D4. Say “same key” or “different key,” then look at the keys.
- **Readiness indicator:** Correctly identifies two of three short pairs with replay available and can point to the changed position after reveal.
- **Later review:** Shape-to-sound in 007, left-hand echoes in 012, phrase comparison in 020, and E001 D in 023–024.

### RF-PULSE-1 — Continuing steady pulse

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 003; direct return 004–006; used in every scored task through 024.
- **Short repeat:** Tap 16 beats while counting four groups of 1–4. Rest for four counted beats and restart at approximately the same speed.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Copy four taps from the optional count model, pause, then copy four more. Use a leg or safe surface rather than keys.
- **Readiness indicator:** Produces 16 beats without an obvious final rush, long unplanned pause, or added beat; perfection to a metronome is not required.
- **Later review:** K001 in 004, rhythm work 005–006, every Play session, and 023–024.

### RF-RHY-1 — One- and two-beat durations

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 005; return 006, 009–010, 014, 018, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** On C4, play `quarter, quarter, half` while counting 1–4. Press once for the half note and release after its second count. Repeat `half, half`.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Use four beat boxes. Tap once in each box; then join boxes 1–2 and hold one tap gesture across them before joining 3–4.
- **Readiness indicator:** Performs at least two R001 patterns with one press per written note and the correct four-beat total.
- **Later review:** R002 in 006, P01 in 010, mixed R004 in 018, and P03 in 021–024.

### RF-RHY-2 — Hear–tap–play rhythm echoes

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 006; listening method returns 012, 018, 024.
- **Short repeat:** Choose one R002 pattern. Hear it once or twice, tap it, reveal the count blocks, and play it twice identically on C4/D4.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Use R001 `quarter, quarter, half` entirely on C4 with spoken count visible; remove audio-comparison pressure.
- **Readiness indicator:** Reproduces three one-measure echoes after no more than two listens each and identifies where the two-beat sound occurs.
- **Later review:** E002 in 012, mixed rhythm cards in 018, and listening/rhythm portions of 024.

### RF-READ-1 — Staff direction and time order

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 007; return in all notation sessions 008–024.
- **Short repeat:** Trace N001 left to right. Say repeat/up/down between notes, then play the shape on C4–E4.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Compare only two large noteheads: same height, one step higher, or one step lower. Move between C4/D4 to match.
- **Readiness indicator:** Maps an unfamiliar four-note contour to same/right/left keyboard motion without needing letter names.
- **Later review:** Treble landmarks 008, treble reading 009–010, bass sequence 013–014, and every score thereafter.

### RF-READ-2 — Treble landmarks C4 and G4

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 008; return 009–010, 020–024.
- **Short repeat:** Shuffle four C4/G4 cards. Point, name, and play; then perform N002 A after a count-in.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Match only C4’s ledger-line note to middle C three times. Add G4 only after C4 is secure.
- **Readiness indicator:** Identifies C4 and G4 from staff position without a mnemonic and plays the correct key in the fixed RH position.
- **Later review:** C4-based step reading 009, M001 in 020, final piece 021–022, and landmarks in 023–024.

### RF-TREBLE-1 — Treble C4–D4–E4 step reading

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 009; apply 010, 018, 020–024.
- **Short repeat:** Preview one N003 card using first note, direction, and rhythm; play after a four-count; reveal names only afterward.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Read two notes only: C4–D4, D4–C4, or C4–C4. Keep RH 1/2 prepared.
- **Readiness indicator:** Plays a new four-note C4–E4 card without letters above notes and with no more than one self-corrected pitch.
- **Later review:** P01 in 010, mixed notation in 018, M001 in 020, and no-hint cards 023–024.

### RF-PIECE-1 — Prepare and repair a complete short piece

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 010; repeat method 016, 021–022, 024.
- **Short repeat:** Choose P01’s least secure measure, speak its count, play it twice, then connect it once to the following measure.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Play only P01 Measures 1–2 after tracing their contour and tapping rhythm. Completion of the whole piece is not required.
- **Readiness indicator:** Uses preview → complete attempt → named repair → replay, and reaches P01’s ending without restarting after every small error.
- **Later review:** P02 preparation in 016 and P03 build/play in 021–022.

### RF-LH-1 — Left-hand five-note setup

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 011; return 012–016, 018, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Find C3 from C4, place LH 5–1 on C3–G3, play K002 once slowly, then release.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Play C3 four relaxed times with LH 5, then add D3 with LH 4. Do not stretch to all five keys.
- **Readiness indicator:** Finds C3 and plays K002 with correct finger order, freely moving wrist, and no pain or sustained tension.
- **Later review:** LH echoes 012, bass landmarks/reading 013–014, and two-hand work 015 onward.

### RF-LH-2 — Left-hand step and echo control

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 012; return 014–016, 024.
- **Short repeat:** Hear E002 A, trace its up/up shape, play C3–D3–E3, then hide the arrows and repeat.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Alternate C3/D3 with LH 5/4 on four beats, first from a visible diagram and then by feel.
- **Readiness indicator:** Keeps an easy LH position through two short patterns and reproduces one three-note echo after optional replay.
- **Later review:** Bass step reading 014, P02 in 016, and listening/playing milestone in 024.

### RF-BASS-1 — Bass landmarks C3 and F3

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 013; return 014, 016, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Shuffle four C3/F3 cards. Point, name, and play; then perform N004 A.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Match only written C3 in the bass second space to LH C3. Add F3 after three accurate C3 matches.
- **Readiness indicator:** Identifies both written landmarks and plays LH 5/2 without shifting the five-note position.
- **Later review:** Step reading in 014, paired score in 016, and landmark checks 023–024.

### RF-BASS-2 — Bass C3–D3–E3 step reading

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 014; apply 016, 018, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Preview one N005 card from C3 and its direction, play after four counts, and reveal names after the attempt.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Read only C3/D3 two-note cards with LH 5/4. Reopen RF-BASS-1 if C3 is not recognized.
- **Readiness indicator:** Plays a new four-note C3–E3 card without written letter names while maintaining the hand position.
- **Later review:** P02 in 016, R004 bass cards in 018, P03 and milestones 021–024.

### RF-HANDS-1 — Alternating hands on a pulse

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 015; return 016, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Tap LH–RH–LH–RH on the legs, then play C3–C4–C3–C4 on four beats with both hands prepared before starting.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Use leg taps only while counting. Then play LH C3 on beat 1 and RH C4 on beat 3, leaving beats 2/4 as counted waits.
- **Readiness indicator:** Switches hands on intended beats without an extra pause, simultaneous note, high lifted waiting hand, or shoulder bracing.
- **Later review:** P02 in 016, P03 M6–7 in 021–022, and full milestone 024.

### RF-HANDS-2 — Paired-staff question and answer

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 016; return 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Play P02 Measures 1–2 only: preview both entrances, perform RH question, keep counting, and give the LH answer.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Play P02 Measure 1, count a full silent four-beat measure with hands prepared, then play Measure 2. Use active-hand labels.
- **Readiness indicator:** Follows top/right and bottom/left lines and changes hands by measure while the pulse continues.
- **Later review:** P03 construction/performance in 021–022 and integrated performance in 024.

### RF-REST-1 — Quarter rest as counted time

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 017; return 018, 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Speak, tap, and play R003 A. Keep saying “2” during its quarter rest and release C4 before that silent beat.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Say 1–4 and clap only on 1 and 3. Then point to a silent beat box on 2 and 4.
- **Readiness indicator:** Leaves exactly one intended silent beat and enters the next note without restarting or shortening the measure.
- **Later review:** R004 in 018, P03 Measure 7 in 021–022, and mixed rhythm 023–024.

### RF-RHY-3 — Mixed rhythm diagnosis

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 018; return 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Identify the one troublesome element—quarter, half, rest, or count-in. Perform that element on one key in two one-measure patterns before restoring the original card.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Build a four-box pulse grid. Put one event in every box, then replace boxes 3–4 with one two-box half note or replace box 2 with a rest.
- **Readiness indicator:** Performs three mixed cards with correct four-beat totals and explains that pulse remains steady while rhythm changes.
- **Later review:** P03 preparation in 021, no-hint mixed card 023, and milestone rhythm 024.

### RF-DYN-1 — Relaxed soft/strong contrast

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 019; apply 020–024.
- **Short repeat:** Listen to D001, play four comfortable soft C4 notes, fully reset, then four comfortably stronger notes. Stop after one contrast to avoid forceful repetition.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Listening only: identify the soft and strong recording. On a non-touch-sensitive keyboard, imitate free motion without demanding a volume change.
- **Readiness indicator:** Produces or identifies two clear levels while shoulder, jaw, fingers, and wrist remain comfortable; stronger playing is not harsh or painful.
- **Later review:** M001 shaping 020, P03 dynamics 021–022, and performance in 024.

### RF-PHRASE-1 — Musical destination and ending

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 020; apply 021–024.
- **Short repeat:** Play M001 evenly, then again leading gently toward G4 and settling on C4 without changing its written rhythm.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Play E4–G4–E4 slowly. Point to G4 as the destination; then add D4–C4 as the ending, with dynamics kept even.
- **Readiness indicator:** Identifies G4 as the phrase destination, C4 as the ending, and creates a subtle shape without adding pauses or changing note values.
- **Later review:** P03 expressive plan in 021–022 and milestone performance 024.

### RF-FINAL-1 — Build P03 in sections

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 021; return 022–024.
- **Short repeat:** Choose one two-measure unit from P03. Tap/count it, play each involved hand alone, then play the unit once in context.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** For Measures 6–7, tap hand labels only: “LH” on beat 1 of M7, wait on beat 2, “RH, RH” on beats 3–4. Then use only C3/C4/D4.
- **Readiness indicator:** Plays Measures 1–4 and 5–8 separately and can name the specific measure/transition needing work.
- **Later review:** Full performances in 022 and 024; targeted no-hint excerpt in 023.

### RF-FINAL-2 — Continue, repair, and replay P03

- **Relevant sessions:** Seed 022; return 023–024.
- **Short repeat:** After one complete attempt, select exactly one measure or transition, practice it twice, connect one neighboring measure, and make one more full attempt.
- **Simpler prerequisite:** Perform only one four-measure phrase. Preserve its setup, count-in, and ending; return to RF-FINAL-1 for any isolated transition.
- **Readiness indicator:** Completes all eight measures with a recognizable pulse, hand order, ending, and planned contrast, continuing through a small error when possible.
- **Later review:** P03 excerpt in 023 and integrated milestone performance in 024.

## Content and safety checks

- A reinforcement card never introduces a pitch, rhythm value, clef, fingering, or coordination pattern before its seed session.
- Every simpler prerequisite reduces one demand: fewer notes, fewer beats, one hand, no notation, or listening without playing.
- Readiness is behavioral and observable but never machine-scored.
- Ear tasks reconnect to visible contour or notation after the learner answers.
- No card asks for pedal, microphone, MIDI, automated recognition, a virtual playable keyboard, or internet audio.
- Repetition volume is deliberately limited where force or tension could accumulate. Pain, numbness, or persistent tension routes to Stop and seek qualified guidance, not “practice more.”
- A qualified piano educator should review all readiness descriptions, physical simplifications, dynamic guidance, and P03 transition supports before implementation.
