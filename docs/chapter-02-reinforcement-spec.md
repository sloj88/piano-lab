# Chapter 02 Reinforcement Specification — Read and Feel the Beat

Status: draft for educator and beginner review. Supports are unnumbered activities.

Ready offers Continue, Repeat, or Keep Playing. Practicing offers one short repeat keyed to the observed difficulty. Stuck opens the simpler prerequisite first and offers the named earlier lesson. Keep the chapter position, completion, earlier readiness, step position and separate understanding/playing checks on every revisit. Offer score or audio answers after a first attempt. Recheck only the relevant observable behavior. Stop physical practice on pain, numbness or persistent tension.

## Applying support

- If a meter is unclear, practice one bar with the count shown. For guessing pitches or losing place, use the clef card or the silent-start routine. For spacing, select the rest, eighth-pair, or tie card that matches what the learner heard in their playing.
- At 052, route by the actual task. Use RF02-START or RF02-READ for an insecure sight start, RF02-TWO/RF02-REST/RF02-PAIR for the clapped rhythm, or the matching piece card for continuity. Save unused C02-052 sight cards for the milestone or Keep Playing.
- Link to Chapter 01 through existing revisit navigation. The learner can return through Home or Continue Learning while retaining the Chapter 02 position.
- Review whether each simplification really removes the demand the learner reported. Trial the permit-to-stop wording, the evidence statements, and the usefulness of one short repeat with an adult beginner and a qualified educator.
