# Chapter 02 Outline — Read and Feel the Beat

Status: curriculum draft for qualified piano educator and adult-beginner review
Sessions: 025–052 (28 core sessions)

Chapter 01 supplied landmarks, C-position fingering in both hands, quarter and half notes, counted silence, and alternating-hand reading. Chapter 02 uses those skills to read unfamiliar music from the page. Its pitch inventory is C3–G3 and C4–G4, within the architecture’s C3–C5 region. Both five-finger positions stay fixed. Readers locate the first note from a landmark, follow repeats and seconds, silently prepare, count in, play, and revisit one specific spot.

| Session | Type and title | Primary objective | Physical evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 025 | Learn · Find your place on the grand staff | Relate the paired staves to middle C and the two familiar positions | Point to the staves, find middle C and lower C, and play one line at a time |
| 026 | Practice · Start without a sound | Prepare a score-based entrance silently | Find the first key and count in, then begin on time |
| 027 | Practice · Read from treble G | Read down from G through F with steps and repeats | Play a new RH line without note labels |
| 028 | Practice · Read from bass F | Read in both directions from bass F | Play a new LH line while keeping the position |
| 029 | Review · Read, glance, return | Recover the place on the score after a brief keyboard glance | Preview, play, and restart at a barline in each clef |
| 030 | Learn · Two beats to a bar | Read and count in 2/4 | Keep two beats in every bar, even on a half note |
| 031 | Practice · Two beats in the left hand | Transfer 2/4 to bass with a counted rest | Enter after a silent beat without adding time |
| 032 | Learn · Three beats to a bar | Read and count in 3/4 | Hold a half note across beats 1–2 and play beat 3 |
| 033 | Learn · One sound for four beats | Read a whole note in 4/4 | Hold one key across the four counted beats |
| 034 | Review · Choose the count-in | Recognize 2/4, 3/4, and 4/4 before starting | Read each signature and count in for one matching bar |
| 035 | Practice · Keep three in the bass | Sustain 3/4 across bass barlines | Play an LH line with steady groups of three |
| 036 | Learn · Two beats of quiet | Read a half rest at its exact beat position | Release, count two silent beats, and re-enter |
| 037 | Review · Hear the missing beats | Hear and locate two counted beats of silence | Match a heard rhythm to one of two scores and play it |
| 038 | Learn · Two notes inside one beat | Divide a quarter beat into a beamed eighth pair | Speak, tap, then play two even attacks within one beat |
| 039 | Practice · Carry the pair through a barline | Keep the pulse across a paired-eighth bar ending | Land on the downbeat after a pair on beat 2 |
| 040 | Practice · Two inside one, left hand | Transfer an eighth pair to bass reading | Play one even LH pair without rushing the next quarter |
| 041 | Review · Hear one attack or two | Hear and locate subdivision on a steady beat | Match what was heard to a score and play the chosen version |
| 042 | Learn · Hold across the barline | Read a tie between identical pitches as one sound | Carry one press across a line without reattacking |
| 043 | Practice · Follow a bass tie | Read a tied quarter across a bass barline in 3/4 | Keep counting through the tie and release before the next note |
| 044 | Review · Rest, repeat, or hold | Choose the correct action at each beat in a mixed line | Make rests silent, repeat separately, and sustain a tie |
| 045 | Practice · Build Window Lights | Prepare the RH piece in two four-bar sections | Read and join both sections from notation |
| 046 | Play · Play Window Lights | Sustain a gentle three-beat phrase from start to finish | Count in, perform, repair one spot, and replay |
| 047 | Practice · Build Footpath | Group the LH piece into two four-bar sections | Keep the pairs inside a beat and silence between attacks where written |
| 048 | Play · Play Footpath | Sustain a clear walking pulse through the LH piece | Perform, repair one spot, and replay |
| 049 | Review · Read a new line in either clef | Transfer the preview routine to new four-bar lines | Read a line in each clef before hearing a model |
| 050 | Play · Two pieces, two characters | Change meter, hand, and musical character between familiar pieces | Play each from its score with a fresh preparation and count-in |
| 051 | Practice · Rehearse your reading routine | Independently select and apply one reading repair | Attempt a fresh line and retry a named trouble spot |
| 052 | Play · Chapter 02 milestone | Independently prepare and read a short unseen score | Clap a new rhythm, sight-play four bars in either clef, and play both pieces |

The three metric challenges are spread across Sessions 030–034. Session 033 returns to familiar 4/4 to teach four-beat sustain. The two new silence and subdivision demands each have separate practice. Ties arrive after learners have already counted through held notes, silent beats, and barlines. Both pieces draw entirely on the taught inventory. The final sight-playing cards keep the pitch shapes and rhythms simpler than the pieces so the learner can demonstrate independent reading.

Suggested implementation slices: 025–032 (navigation of score and meter), 033–040 (duration, silence, subdivision), 041–048 (listening, ties, repertoire), 049–052 (transfer and milestone).
